# The G.R.A.C.E. Framework

G.R.A.C.E. is a set of measurable indicators that lets PLC Automation capture and communicate the value of extending asset life and reusing components.

| Letter | Pillar |
|---|---|
| G | Gratification (satisfaction) |
| R | Recycling |
| A | Accountability |
| C | Cost & downtime savings |
| E | Experience (communication) |

---

## G: Gratification

| Metric | Purpose | Example display |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer satisfaction %** | Measures client trust; gauges service experience | "85% of clients felt that …" |
| **Employee satisfaction %** | Reflects productivity and operational efficiency | "85% of employees felt that …" |
| **Repeat purchase rate** | Indicates customer retention; signals revenue stability | "In Jan 2026, X% of customers came back after their first purchase." |

The repeat purchase rate was piloted on a month of anonymised order data.

## R: Recycling

| Metric | Definition | Demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| **Recovery rate (%)** | % of parts salvaged instead of discarded | Less material waste, efficient use of resources, strong environmental positioning |

## A: Accountability

| Metric | Purpose | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Product quality management**: warranty claim rate and improvement rate (how the claim rate changes over time) | Make sure products meet the standard | Lower rework cost, shows product reliability, builds long-term trust |
| **Complaint resolution rate**: % of complaints successfully resolved | Show responsiveness | Stops issues escalating, protects brand reputation |

## C: Cost & downtime savings

The quantitative core of the project. Two complementary views:

- **Time savings:** e.g. "PLC Automation saved its clients N hours of downtime this month."
- **Cost savings:** e.g. "PLC Automation helped clients avoid ~$X of downtime exposure this month."

Both can be tracked as a trend over time. Full model in [methodology.md](methodology.md).

## E: Experience

How the measured impact reaches customers and stakeholders:

| Channel | Current state | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| **Website** | Describes services; no performance data; generic call to action; company-focused | Outcome-led hero ("Extending asset lifespan. Reducing downtime. Driving sustainability."); quantified impact tiles; call to action tied to impact ("View our impact", downtime estimator) |
| **Reports** | n/a | Downloadable operational impact / sustainability report from the contact page with downtime, cost and recovery breakdowns. It can be printed as a client pamphlet |
| **Social media** | n/a | Visual impact posts. Widens the reach of sustainability work and builds credibility through regular, transparent updates |

Identified gaps in the existing website: ESG metrics are not shown, sustainability impact is not clearly communicated, and benefits are implied rather than backed by numbers.
